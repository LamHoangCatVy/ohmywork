# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ohmywork a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, experiences, and technical choices.
- Give and accept constructive, specific feedback.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn when a mistake causes harm.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Retaliation against good-faith safety, conduct, or quality reports.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that conflict with this policy. They may issue a private warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban proportional to context, impact, and pattern of behavior.

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not put sensitive personal information in a public issue. If a confidential maintainer channel is not available or a maintainer is involved, use [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/) and preserve only the evidence necessary for review.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters as far as reasonably possible. Bad-faith reports and retaliation are themselves violations.
